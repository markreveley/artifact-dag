A proposition is the content of a declarative sentence: what is the case if the sentence is true.

## Sub-definitions

### Frege (1892)
The thought expressed by a sentence, distinct from the sentence itself and from its truth value.

### Stalnaker (1976)
A set of possible worlds, namely those in which the sentence is true.

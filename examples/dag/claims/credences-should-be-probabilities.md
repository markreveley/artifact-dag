title: Credences should obey the probability axioms
subject: def:credence
tags: [epistemics]
sources:
  - Ramsey 1926, "Truth and Probability"
  - de Finetti 1937
uses: [def:credence]
---
An agent whose credences violate the probability axioms can be offered a set of bets they will each accept and that together guarantee a loss (a Dutch book).

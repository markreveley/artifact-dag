---
id: claim:beliefs-are-not-voluntary
type: claim
subtype: proposition
title: Beliefs cannot be adopted at will
subject: def:belief
tags: [epistemics]
sources:
  - Williams 1973, "Deciding to Believe"
uses: [def:belief]
---
An agent cannot acquire a [[def:belief]] simply by deciding to, because believing involves taking the proposition to be true, and one cannot take something to be true while knowing one adopted it for reasons unrelated to its truth.

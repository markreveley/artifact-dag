---
id: claim:evidence-can-be-misleading
type: claim
subtype: observation
title: Evidence can raise credence in a falsehood
subject: def:evidence
tags: [epistemics]
uses: [def:evidence, def:conditionalization]
contradicts: [claim:calibration-is-a-virtue]
---
Since evidence is defined by its effect on rational credence rather than by truth, a sequence of genuine evidence can lead a perfectly conditionalizing agent to high credence in a false proposition, as with [[claim:simpsons-paradox-example]].
